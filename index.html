FAQs

Questions learners commonly ask

Which safety courses do you offer?

Popular options include NEBOSH IGC, IOSH Managing Safely, OSHA, First Aid, Risk Assessment, Fire Safety and additional HSE short courses.

Do you offer international qualifications?

Yes. AASH International promotes a range of professional and vocational qualifications, including ProQual and ICTQual programmes shown on this website. Contact us for current awarding-body and programme details.

Can I study online?

Online and on-site delivery may be available depending on the course and intake. Contact the academy before enrolment to confirm the current delivery arrangement.

Are all courses available all year?

Not necessarily. Course availability, intake dates, entry requirements and delivery arrangements can vary. Please enquire for the latest schedule.
